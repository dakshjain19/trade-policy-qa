NIRMAL FOODS PVT LTD
TRADE PROMOTION, CLAIMS AND APPROVALS POLICY
Version 2.0 | Effective date: 1 April 2025 | Owner: Sales Operations

SECTION 1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE
This policy sets the rules for trade discounts, promotional schemes,
retailer claims and distributor credit at Nirmal Foods Pvt Ltd. It
applies to all field sales staff, Area Sales Managers, Regional Sales
Managers and appointed distributors across India. It does not cover
employee travel, payroll, leave or any other HR matter. Those are
covered in separate HR policies.

SECTION 2. DEFINITIONS
2.1 General Trade (GT): independent kirana stores, small grocers and
wholesalers served through distributors.
2.2 Modern Trade (MT): organised retail chains such as supermarkets and
hypermarkets, with centralised buying.
2.3 E-commerce (EC): online marketplaces and quick-commerce platforms.
2.4 Scheme: any time-bound promotional offer, including price-offs,
bundle offers, free goods and display incentives.
2.5 ASM: Area Sales Manager. RSM: Regional Sales Manager.

SECTION 3. MAXIMUM TRADE DISCOUNT BY CHANNEL
The maximum trade discount that may be offered on invoice value is
given below. Any discount above these limits is not permitted, even
with approval.

| Channel          | Maximum discount | Notes                              |
|------------------|------------------|------------------------------------|
| General Trade    | 8%               | Applies at distributor invoice     |
| Modern Trade     | 12%              | Includes listing and margin support|
| E-commerce       | 10%              | Excludes platform-funded offers    |

3.1 Revision note: In Version 1.0 (2024), the Modern Trade limit was
15%. From 1 April 2025 the Modern Trade limit is reduced to 12%.
All new Modern Trade agreements must follow the 12% limit.

SECTION 4. SCHEME APPROVAL MATRIX
Every scheme must be approved before launch. The approver depends on
the total estimated scheme value.
4.1 Scheme value up to Rs 1 lakh: approved by the Area Sales Manager.
4.2 Scheme value above Rs 1 lakh and up to Rs 5 lakh: approved by the
Regional Sales Manager.
4.3 Scheme value above Rs 5 lakh: joint approval by the Head of Sales
and the Finance Controller.
4.4 Schemes must be submitted at least 10 working days before the
planned launch date.
4.5 A scheme launched without approval will not be reimbursed.

SECTION 5. RETAILER CLAIMS
5.1 Damaged goods: the retailer must file a claim within 15 days of
the delivery date. Claims must include photographs of the damaged
stock and the original invoice number.
5.2 Near-expiry stock: stock may be returned if it is within 30 days
of its expiry date. Returns are accepted only for General Trade.
5.3 Short supply: shortfalls must be noted on the delivery challan at
the time of delivery. Later claims for short supply are rejected.
5.4 Settlement: approved claims are settled as a credit note within
45 days of claim approval.
5.5 Claims above Rs 50,000 require verification by the RSM before
approval.

SECTION 6. DISPLAY AND VISIBILITY INCENTIVES
6.1 Display rental for a premium shelf or end-cap may be paid to
Modern Trade stores only.
6.2 Display rental is capped at Rs 5,000 per store per month.
6.3 Payment is released only after photo proof of the display is
uploaded to the sales app for each month.

SECTION 7. DISTRIBUTOR CREDIT TERMS
7.1 General Trade distributors receive a credit period of 21 days.
7.2 Modern Trade distributors receive a credit period of 30 days.
7.3 If a distributor's overdue amount exceeds 60 days, fresh supplies
are put on hold until the overdue amount is cleared.

SECTION 8. NON-COMPLIANCE
8.1 Any breach of this policy must be reported to Sales Operations.
8.2 Repeated breaches may lead to recovery of the excess discount from
the distributor and disciplinary action for the staff involved.
